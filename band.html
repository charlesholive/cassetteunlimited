Meet the Band

The faces behind the sound

Virginie Marine
Lead Vocals

Born in Belgium, raised in southern France, and now electrifying stages across New York City. Virginie is a force of nature with a rare, sweeping vocal range and over 25 years of live performance across the U.S. and Europe. She can channel Céline Dion one moment and Ann Wilson of Heart the next, with a magnetic stage presence and an uncanny ability to connect with every person in the room. From pop anthems and soulful ballads to hard-hitting rock and disco floor-fillers, there’s no genre she can’t own. A two-time album artist and winner of multiple singing competitions, Virginie performs in English, French, Spanish, and Italian.

Juliet Markowitz
Lead Vocals

A versatile NYC-based singer, actress, and cabaret artist, Juliet brings stage presence and vocal depth to every performance. Her theatrical background and dynamic range make her the perfect partner for the band's biggest anthems.

Matt Scharfglass
Bass

Award-winning multi-instrumentalist who's played everything from symphonic metal with Trans-Siberian Orchestra to R&B with Ashford & Simpson. Matt is also the guitarist/bassist for New York Rangers home games at Madison Square Garden and the author of a dozen bass and guitar instructional books.

Tom Curiano
Drums & Production

Veteran NYC drummer with extensive touring and recording credits. A long-standing member of the Garland Jeffreys Band, Tom has performed across the U.S., Europe, and Japan with artists including James Maddock, Dana Fuchs, and Popa Chubby. He recently recorded drums for an upcoming album by Ariel Bender of Mott the Hoople. Also a multi-instrumentalist and producer with six independent records to his name.

Chris Chia
Guitar

Surgeon by day, guitarist by night. Chris brings precision and passion to every riff, proving that rock & roll knows no boundaries.

Adam Narimatsu
Keys & Piano

A premiere pianist on the NYC scene, Adam blends jazz, blues, and funk with classical chops. Performing professionally since 2011, he's graced stages at the Empire State Building, Plaza Hotel, Lincoln Center, and Bowery Ballroom. Influenced by Oscar Peterson, Brad Mehldau, and Cory Henry.
